# QDFH
The _Questionario Digitale de Fonètica Històrica (QDFH)_ is an enhanced digital edition of the _Questionnaire de fonétique historique_ located as an appendix of the _Atlas Linguistique Roman (ALiR)_ (Tuaillon & Contini, 1996-2009). The phonetic survey is a list of 284 phonetic contexts in Latin—both for consonants and vowels—alongside example words.

The intended scope of this digital survey is ¹ to provide the Romance language outcomes of more varieties than just the usual biggest five or so, and ² to provide a greater level of specificity than is usually provided in many sources.
