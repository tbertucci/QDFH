# Questionario Digitale de Fonètica Històrica (QDFH)
The _Questionario Digitale de Fonètica Històrica (QDFH)_ is an enhanced digital edition of the _Questionnaire de fonétique historique_, an appendix of the _Atlas Linguistique Roman (ALiR)_ (Tuaillon & Contini, 1996-2009). The phonetic survey is a list of 284 phonetic contexts in Latin—both for consonants and vowels—alongside example words.

The intended scope of this digital survey database is ¹ to provide the Romance language outcomes of more varieties than just the usual biggest five or so, ² to provide a greater level of specificity than is usually provided in many sources, and ³ to keep this information open-access and collaborative. At the time of first publishing to GitHub, 32 language varieties (from 24 languages) are included in the database. The creator of the project, Tim Bertucci, has also developed a modified version of the International Phonetic Alphabet chart which is also available within the files. Many thanks are in order for Leigha DeRango who carefully collaborated on the project, setting up the Python code for the interactive map application.
